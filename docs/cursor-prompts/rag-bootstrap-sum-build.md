WHAT BROKE. On Tue 10 Nov 2026 the PM seat ran python3 -m scorer.rag report on step 9's two A/B runs, traces/w4-a and traces/w4-b, which are not on main yet. report.md came out byte-identical on Python 3.9.6 and 3.14.7, and reports/rag.json did not: five of the scorers' means differ in their last digit, for example faithfulness in variant a, at 0.5010416666666666 on 3.9 and 0.5010416666666667 on 3.14. The cause is paired_bootstrap in scorer/stats.py. It computes its observed mean with the built-in sum(), and from Python 3.12 sum() adds floats with compensated summation, so the result depends on the interpreter. Its resample means add with an explicit +=, and those agree on both. Once the runs, the statement and the report are committed, tests.test_rag_harness.ReportTests.test_the_committed_config_reports_only_once_both_runs_exist can pass on only one of verify's two legs. The PM seat saw it fail on 3.9 and pass on 3.14 with the 3.14 output. You can reproduce the cause on main without the runs: python3 -c "from scorer.stats import paired_bootstrap; print(repr(paired_bootstrap([0.0]*10, [0.1]*10, n_boot=1, seed=7)[0]))" prints 0.09999999999999999 on 3.9 and 0.1 on 3.14.

THE CHANGE. (1) In paired_bootstrap, compute the observed mean by adding the differences left to right with +=, in input order, then dividing by their count, as each resample already does. On every interpreter this gives what 3.9's sum() gives, so the observed mean and the resample means use the same arithmetic, and nothing a 3.9 run produced changes. Say in the docstring that it adds left to right so its bytes do not depend on the Python version. (2) One test in tests/test_rag_bootstrap.py: paired_bootstrap([0.0]*10, [0.1]*10, n_boot=1, seed=7)[0] equals 0.09999999999999999, and bootstrap_mean([0.1]*10, 1, 7)[0] equals the same. Against main's code it must fail on 3.14. (3) Read every sum() and every other float accumulation on the path of python3 -m scorer.rag report and of uploaders/braintrust_eval.py, and name in the PR body any other one whose result could depend on the interpreter. The PM seat read scorer/rag.py and found its sums over ints and bools only. Change no other one without stopping to ask.
